A virtual office for your team

Bump into someone.
Just start talking.

engawa is a virtual office that runs entirely in the browser. Move your avatar around a 2D map — walk up to a colleague and voice, video, and screen-share connect automatically. Step away and they quietly disconnect.

View on GitHub How it works
An engawa is the wooden veranda between the inside and the outside of a traditional Japanese house.
A place you could sit without any particular reason, and trade a few words with whoever happened to pass by.
engawa brings that back to the screen — the casual run-ins that remote work quietly takes away.

Features

Get closer, and you're connected.

The "just say hi" feeling of a Slack huddle or Gather.town — made light.

🔊

Proximity voice

Voice connects automatically as you approach, and drops as you leave.

📷

Video

Turn on your camera. The active speaker's frame gently glows.

🖥

Screen share

Share in one click. Switch between inset, side panel, and full screen.

⏺

Recording

Record the floor, shares, every camera and everyone's audio — composited in the browser.

🧍

Avatars

Build your own pixel-art avatar — hair, clothes, skin tone and more.

🟢

Status

Show whether you're online, busy, or away.

👥

Steady calls

Calls of two or more go through an SFU by default, so one shaky connection doesn't drag everyone else down.

📶

Adapts to your network

When the network struggles, video steps down first to keep voice clear. Drops reconnect on their own.

🎧

Noise suppression

Keyboard clatter and fan hum are removed in the browser, and your mic goes silent between sentences.

🚪

Auto-off on leaving

Step out of a meeting room and your mic, camera and screen share switch off.

🔒

Access password

Set a password so only your team can get in — or leave it open.

📲

Installable (PWA)

Install on Chrome / Edge and launch it in its own window.

Make it yours

Design your own avatar.

Tune gender, skin tone, hair, clothes, hats and glasses. The pixel art is built on LPC (Liberated Pixel Cup) — so everyone shows up as their own character.

How it works

Your media never
touches our server.

Voice, video and screen-share flow through Cloudflare Realtime SFU for any call of two or more — or straight over WebRTC P2P if you don't configure an SFU. engawa's own server only brokers introductions and syncs positions. That's what keeps it light and cheap.

  1. 1

    Only the introductions

    It figures out who's near whom and relays WebRTC signaling.

  2. 2

    Media goes P2P / SFU

    The actual audio/video flows straight through Cloudflare's SFU, or peer-to-peer.

  3. 3

    Stateless by design

    No database. A simple server that resets clean on restart.
